# Daily Posts (admin) — Manual Test Script

> Written by hand from §1.18 of `ctf/docs/developer/ctf-plugin-feature-inventories/ctf-non-plugin-feature-inventory.md`.
> Daily Posts is not a plugin, so it has no row in `ctf/config/manual-test-script-manifest.json` and is not
> regenerated by `test-script:generate`. Update this file by hand when §1.18 changes.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Area** | Daily Posts (`/admin/daily-posts`), non-plugin admin screen |
| **Visibility** | Admin only |
| **Roles to test** | admin, approved member |
| **Surfaces** | Web (`/admin/daily-posts`); Android: out of scope (web-only per rule 105) |
| **Seed first** | none; the posts come from the blog's `posts.json` |

---

### DP-1 — The tile opens the day's list

**Role:** Admin
**Steps:**
1. Open `/admin`.
2. Tap **Daily Posts**.

**Expected:**
- Step 1: there is a Daily Posts row and no Quora Message of the Day row.
- Step 2: the screen shows today's date and five sections in order: Today, PB2 Message of the Day, Today's blog posts, Templates, Themes.

### DP-1a — Today: the live topic, the blurbs and the platform ticks

**Role:** Admin
**Steps:**
1. Look at the Today section.
2. Tap **Copy** on the live topic and paste into a note.
3. Tap **Copy** on the first blurb and paste into a note.
4. Tap the **Quora** row under Platforms, then reload the screen.
5. Tap the **Quora** row again, then reload.
6. Open the screen again the next day (Eastern).

**Expected:**
- Step 1: a live topic labeled with its line, with three talking points; four blurbs, one per line (Peace Battle 2, Peace Battle 2 conference, Finding skills and One Percent, Why I am not on Quora); and eleven platform rows in three groups (Full posts, Blurbs, Lives and videos), with a count of how many are done. No row names a part of the app.
- Step 2: the note holds the title, the three points as a list, and the link on its own line; the topic shows a green tick.
- Step 3: the note holds the blurb and the link on its own line; the blurb shows a green tick.
- Step 4: Quora shows a green tick and the count goes up by one after the reload.
- Step 5: the tick is gone after the reload.
- Step 6: the live topic is from the next line, the blurbs have moved on, and no platform is ticked.

### DP-2 — Copy ticks an item off for today, and the tick can be taken back

**Role:** Admin
**Steps:**
1. Under Today's blog posts, tap **Copy** on the first row and paste into a note.
2. Reload the screen.
3. Tap the green tick on the same row.
4. Reload the screen.

**Expected:**
- Step 1: the note holds the post's title, a blank line, the full post, a blank line, and `Full post: <address>` on its own line. The row shows a green tick.
- Step 2: the tick is still there.
- Step 3–4: the tick is gone after the reload.

### DP-3 — The rotation shows a different share of posts each day

**Role:** Admin
**Steps:**
1. Note the post numbers under Today's blog posts.
2. Open the screen again the next day (Eastern).

**Expected:**
- Step 1: the rows show only `No. N`, about one thirtieth of the blog's posts (five while there are 150).
- Step 2: none of the numbers repeat from the day before.

### DP-4 — PB2 Message of the Day

**Role:** Admin
**Steps:**
1. Tap **Copy** under PB2 Message of the Day and paste into a note.
2. Tap **See the coming days**.
3. Use the back control.

**Expected:**
- Step 1: the note holds the day line, the title and the body, matching the copy on the PB2 page.
- Step 2: the PB2 page opens with the header PB2 Message of the Day.
- Step 3: back returns to Daily Posts.

### DP-5 — Templates can be added, copied, edited and deleted

**Role:** Admin
**Steps:**
1. Tap **Add a template**, enter a made-up title and text, and tap **Save template**.
2. Tap **Copy** on the new row and paste into a note.
3. Tap **Edit**, change the title, and tap **Save changes**.
4. Tap **Delete** and confirm.
5. Tap **Add a template** and tap **Save template** with the title empty.

**Expected:**
- Step 1: a row with the title and a Copy button appears.
- Step 2: the note holds the text exactly as entered; the row shows a green tick.
- Step 3: the row shows the new title.
- Step 4: the row is gone, and stays gone after a reload.
- Step 5: the form says "A template needs a title." and nothing is saved.

### DP-6 — Theme groups and Next

**Role:** Admin
**Steps:**
1. Open **TIs with reliable rides** under Themes.
2. Tap **Copy** on the row marked Next, then reload the screen and open the same group.
3. On any row, tap **Groups**, tick a second group, and tap **Save**.
4. Open that second group.
5. Tap **Groups** on the same row, then **Use the post's links**.

**Expected:**
- Step 1: the group lists posts that link TrustTransport, each as `No. N`, 15 to a page with Previous and Next page when there are more.
- Step 2: the copied row shows today's tick, and Next has moved to the post after it.
- Step 3–4: the post now appears in both groups, and its Groups link says "(set by hand)".
- Step 5: the post is back in only the groups its own links give.

### DP-6a — Placing an Unsorted post

**Role:** Admin
**Steps:**
1. Find Unsorted at the top of Themes (shown only while some post links no part of the app).
2. On one post, tick a group and tap **Save**.

**Expected:**
- Step 1: Unsorted is already open, and every post in it shows its title and the ten group choices.
- Step 2: the post leaves Unsorted and appears in the ticked group.

### DP-6b — Leaving a post out of the rotation

**Role:** Admin
**Steps:**
1. Under Today's blog posts, tap **Leave out of rotation** on one post.
2. In Themes, open **Groups** on another post and tap **Leave out of rotation (it stays on the blog)**.
3. Scroll to **Left out** at the bottom and tap **Bring back** on the first one.
4. Open Today in the app on a day one of the left-out posts was due as a story.

**Expected:**
- Steps 1 and 2: the post disappears from Today's blog posts and from every theme group, and appears under Left out with its number and title. It is still on the blog.
- Step 3: the post leaves Left out and is back in its groups, and in Today's blog posts on its own day.
- Step 4: that day shows one story card fewer; the other stories are the same as before.
